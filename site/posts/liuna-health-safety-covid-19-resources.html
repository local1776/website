LHSFNA's COVID-19 Website Offers Comprehensive Resources

LIUNA's Health and Safety Fund of North America (LHSFNA) is committed to the safety of our members and the employees we represent. This commitment is more important than ever as a result of the COVID-19. In an effort to support the safety of ongoing operations, or the reopening of work facilities after they were shutdown for COVID-19, LHSFNA has developed a number of different products and materials to assist employers and employees in mitigating the spread of COVID-19 at work and beyond in order to protect our worker's families and dependents.
LHSFNA has made available these materials on their website at their COVID-19 page located here:
https://www.lhsfna.org/index.cfm/coronavirus/
We encourage you to visit their site and take a look at the many resources available. Please make sure to come back and visit to see updates as the situation develops.
If you have any questions or concerns about a particular COVID-19-related situation at your worksite, please do not hesitate to contact us .
