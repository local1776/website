Are You Facing a Financial Hardship?

Local 1776 charges a retainer because representing you has real costs, such as legal help, filing and travel. A hardship waiver is for people who would suffer serious financial harm by paying it. To show serious financial harm, you must generally be able to show that you expect to be in a non-paid status for at least 80 hours, in a row or added up, during the next 90 days, and that you have no other leave available to use. We also look at your whole household: other income, bills and savings. Being suspended or removed does not by itself mean a hardship. A request without proof is not considered.
