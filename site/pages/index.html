LIUNA Local 1776

We Represent the Federal Employees Who Keep the National Guard Running.

Local 1776 handles federal workplace matters for U.S. government employees, including civilian employees of the Department of Defense and the Army and Air National Guard. We are part of the Laborers’ International Union of North America, one of the largest and strongest unions in the U.S. and Canada.
