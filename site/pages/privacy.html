Privacy Policy

What this website collects, where it goes, who sees it, and what it never does.

In Brief

The Short Version

  • We don’t track you. This website has no advertising, no visitor analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • What you send goes to Local 1776 and the services that help us handle it. When you send a form, it goes to Local 1776’s secure membership system and to our staff by email. Nothing stays on this website once you press Send.{{IF:representation}} If you use Save for Later on the Representation Request, your unsent answers stay only in your own browser until you press Send.{{ELSE}}{{END}}
  • Some tools never send anything. The AGR Pay Calculator does its math in your browser and keeps nothing, and the Emergency Leave Tracker saves what you type only in your own browser.
  • We never sell your information. Apart from our attorneys and the services that help us handle it, we share it only with your permission or when the law requires it. We use an artificial intelligence service to help us read and organize requests and documents, only to represent you.
Representation, Uniforms and Contact Us

Forms You Send Us

What we receive. Only what you type into the form: for example your name, contact details, job information, and what is going on at work. On the Representation Request this can include medical information and the details of a hardship request.

Where it goes. Your request goes to Local 1776’s secure membership system, which runs on Google Firebase, and by email (through our email service, Brevo) to Local 1776 staff, with a copy to you. The website itself keeps no copy.

Who sees it. Local 1776 staff and representatives who need it to work on your request, and, when your case calls for it, the attorneys Local 1776 retains. We examine the records you send us and the records your agency gives us with your permission. Apart from our attorneys and the services that help us handle your request, we do not share your records with anyone without your permission, unless the law requires it. Part of our review sometimes involves the use of artificial intelligence (AI). To see whether you are a member, we compare your name, email and phone number with our membership records.

How we use artificial intelligence. An artificial intelligence service helps our staff read and summarize representation requests and case documents, including any medical or personnel details in them. It does not decide anything. A person reviews every request, and only the Business Manager accepts or declines it.

What we share with your agency. When we represent you, we give your agency the Designation of Representation you sign, with your name, your job and the general topic of your request. We share anything more only with your permission. We request your personnel and medical records from your agency only with the permission you give on the form.

How long we keep it. As long as we need it to represent you, and as long as the law requires us to keep union records.

Documents you email us at documents@local1776.org are handled the same way.

Nothing Leaves Your Device

Tools That Stay on Your Computer or Phone

The Emergency Leave Tracker saves what you type only in your own browser, so it is there when you come back on the same device. It is never sent to Local 1776 or anyone else. To erase it, press Clear on the tracker or clear your browser’s site data.

The Request Representation form{{IF:representation}} has{{ELSE}}, when it opens, will have{{END}} a Save for Later button. It keeps your answers only in your own browser, never your signature, so you can finish later on the same device. Nothing is sent to Local 1776 until you press Send. The saved copy is erased when you send the form, when you press Restart, the next time you open the form after 30 days, or when you clear your browser’s site data.

The AGR Pay Calculator does all its math in your browser. What you type is never sent to Local 1776 or anyone else, and it is not saved: close the page and it is gone. Print / Save as PDF makes the file on your own device.

Outside Services

What Your Browser Connects To

Like any website, some pages load pieces from other services. Each one receives your internet (IP) address, as every website you visit does. None of them receives anything you type, except where noted below.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • The U.S. Census Bureau receives the address you type into “Find Your Members of Congress” or Take Action, to look up your congressional district. It goes straight to the Census Bureau; Local 1776 never sees or keeps it.
  • Local 1776’s membership system (Google Firebase) supplies the public member counts and legislative information on the Legislation page. Your browser only reads them; nothing about you is sent.
  • YouTube plays our videos in its privacy-enhanced mode, and only after you press play.
  • Google Maps opens only when you tap Directions on a work site.
  • PayPal handles membership dues when you join. PayPal’s own privacy policy applies there, and Local 1776 never sees your card or bank details.

Links to other websites, such as Congress.gov, government agencies and news sites, follow those sites’ own privacy policies.

Cookies, Children and Changes

The Fine Print

Cookies. This website sets no cookies. YouTube and PayPal may set their own once you play a video or go to PayPal.

Children. This website is for adults and is not meant for children under 13.

Your choices. You can ask what information Local 1776 holds about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Use the contact details below.

Changes. When this policy changes, we post the new version here with a new date at the top.
