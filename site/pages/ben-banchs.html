Full-Time Staff

Ben Banchs

Business Manager and Secretary-Treasurer of LIUNA Local 1776 since December 2010.

Ben Banchs has led LIUNA Local 1776 as Business Manager and Secretary-Treasurer since December 2010. Originally from Ponce, Puerto Rico, he joined LIUNA in 2005, when he was a dual-status technician himself, so he knows the job our members do from the inside.

From the Flight Line to the Bargaining Table

Mr. Banchs served 27 years in the Louisiana Air National Guard, from 1991 to 2018, and retired as a Master Sergeant. He worked on F-15 armament systems and then F-15 avionics. From 1997 to 2001 he spent four and a half years in Khamis Mushayt, Saudi Arabia, as a defense contractor training the Royal Saudi Air Force, working for McDonnell Douglas, Boeing and Al Salam Aircraft. From 2003 to 2009 he was a full-time technician, an F-15 electronics integrated systems mechanic, and from 2009 to 2010 a labor relations specialist for the Louisiana National Guard. He has seen the work from the shop floor and from management’s side of the table.

After Hurricane Katrina in 2005, he served six months on active duty, taking on every kind of emergency work: security, humanitarian relief and the recovery of remains. When the storm drove up the cost of living in Southeast Louisiana, he was primarily responsible for winning a 30% pay increase for the area’s blue-collar federal employees. From 2006 to about 2014 he served on the Local Wage Survey Committee for the New Orleans federal wage survey (area 061).

During the August 2016 floods in Baton Rouge, he set his avionics training aside to help evacuees: he moved special-needs evacuees by bus from the shelter to a temporary medical facility near Louisiana State University.

Speaking for Technicians

As Business Manager he speaks for technicians in Washington and in the courts.

  • In July 2015 he testified before the National Commission on the Future of the Army that the Guard’s success rests on its small, experienced and affordable technician workforce.
  • He has long argued that dual-status technicians should be Title 5 civil servants, so that keeping a civilian job no longer depends on staying in the military. He made the case on the America’s Work Force Union Podcast in 2022.
  • He was the union representative in the dispute that led to FLRA v. Michigan Army National Guard (Sixth Circuit, 2017). That ruling upheld technicians’ right to talk with their union in private.
  • He represented the employee in Erdel v. Department of the Army (2023 MSPB 27). That decision established that the MSPB can order relief for a fired technician.

He has written 39 of the stories in Local 1776’s news, most of its commentary on federal workforce issues.

Beyond the Union

Mr. Banchs has been happily married for more than 30 years. He and his wife have three beautiful daughters, and he is a proud new grandfather to their first granddaughter.

In November 2026 Mr. Banchs ran for the St. Tammany Parish School Board. He came up short, but he calls it a very enlightening experience and a continuation of his public service.

He also performs with three bands: Hard 8, a New Orleans rock, blues and country cover band, and two Mandeville bands, Fontaineblues and Meteorite.
