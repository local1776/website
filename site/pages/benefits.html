Benefits

Membership Has Its Privileges

Local 1776 builds value into your membership with programs that protect you at work and save your family money. As union members, you and your family can also use Union Plus, a nonprofit created by the AFL-CIO to bring savings and support to working families.

From Local 1776

Member-Only Programs

01 · Advice

Employment Advice

Every member can get unlimited advice on general matters about their civilian job or their military membership in the National Guard, from experienced Local 1776 representatives.

Find Your Representative ›
02 · Legal

Legal Services

For more complex issues, members can consult our retained law firm, Minahan Muther Klinger PC. Depending on the merits, members may qualify for representation on a contingency or discounted basis.

Visit the Law Firm ›
03 · California

Dental and Vision Plans

California members can enroll in dental and vision plans through Local 1776. Contact us to sign up and we will walk you through your options.

Union Plus Hardship Help

Furloughed, Laid Off or Working Through a Shutdown?

Union Plus offers grants that do not have to be repaid to eligible members hurt by a government shutdown, a layoff or a furlough, along with mortgage help, disaster relief and free credit counseling.
