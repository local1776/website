Members and Non-Members

When We Represent Everyone

The union is obligated to represent all employees, members or not, in only four situations:

  1. Contract negotiations
  2. Grievances and arbitration (based on the merits of the individual case)
  3. Weingarten and investigatory examinations
  4. Formal meetings and discussions

For everything else, Local 1776 decides whether to represent an employee on the merits of the case, not on whether they are a member.

Members and non-members alike may be responsible for the costs of representation described below.

Arbitration, Hearings, and Attorney Fees

LIUNA Local 1776 decides whether a case moves forward to arbitration, an Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) hearing, or an appeal to the Merit Systems Protection Board (MSPB). The union also decides whether to bring in an attorney. Attorney fees can come up at any of these stages, not only in arbitration. Who pays those fees (you, the union, or both) and how much each pays is decided case by case, based on the strength of the case. This applies equally to members and non-members.

When the Union Declines or Ends Representation

LIUNA Local 1776 alone decides whether to accept a case and whether to continue with it. That decision is based on the merits of the case, meaning how strong it is. It is never based on whether you are a member or on who you are. The union may end its representation if, for example, you have been offered a fair settlement or the best settlement you are likely to get, or if the union concludes the case cannot be won. The union may also end its representation if you withhold information or are not truthful with us.

Representation Fee on Monetary Awards

When LIUNA Local 1776 obtains a monetary settlement or award for you, the union charges a representation fee of 10% of the total amount, calculated before taxes. The fee applies whether money is the entire result of your case or only part of it. The agency pays the settlement to the union. The union retains its 10% representation fee and pays the remaining 90% to you. For example, on a $50,000 settlement, the union retains $5,000 as its representation fee and you receive $45,000. This fee is separate from, and in addition to, any other costs or fees, including attorney fees.

No Guarantee of Results

Choosing a representative is an important decision. Every case is different, and the outcome depends on many factors, including the facts and the merits of the case. LIUNA Local 1776 and its representatives cannot guarantee or promise any particular outcome. Results in past cases do not guarantee a similar result in yours.

What You Acknowledge

1. Your Application Generates a Standard Form 1187

That form contains the following information:

Privacy Act

Section 5525 of Title 5 United States Code (Allotments and Assignments of Pay) permits Federal agencies to collect this information. This completed form is used to request that labor organization dues be deducted from your pay and to notify your labor organization of the deduction. Completing this form is voluntary, but it may not be processed if all requested information is not provided.

This record may be disclosed outside your agency to: 1) the Department of the Treasury to make proper financial adjustments; 2) a Congressional office if you make an inquiry to that office related to this record; 3) a court or an appropriate Government agency if the Government is party to a legal suit; 4) an appropriate law enforcement agency if we become aware of a legal violation; 5) an organization which is a designated collection agent of a particular labor organization; and 6) other Federal agencies for management, statistical and other official functions (without your personal identification).

Executive Order 9397 allows Federal agencies to use the social security number (SSN) as an individual identifier to avoid confusion caused by employees with the same or similar names. Supplying your SSN is voluntary, but failure to provide it, when it is used as the employee identification number, may mean that payroll deductions cannot be processed.

Your agency shall provide an additional statement if it uses the information furnished on this form for purposes other than those mentioned above.

I hereby authorize the above-named agency to deduct from my pay each pay period, or the first full pay period of each month, the amount certified above as the regular dues of the (Name of Labor Organization): LIUNA; and to remit such amount to that labor organization in accordance with its arrangements with my employing agency. 2. Recruitment Incentive Acknowledgment (Gift Card, When Available)

The individual affirms that they and their recruiter (if any) are employed in a bargaining unit covered by LIUNA Local 1776, or are designated organizers of Local 1776. To be eligible for organizing incentives, a new member agrees to pay LIUNA Local 1776 dues for at least one year, or an amount equal to the monthly dues in effect when they joined multiplied by twelve. Once the union verifies that dues are being collected, both the new member and their recruiter are eligible for a monetary incentive for taking part in a sanctioned Local 1776 organizing campaign. Money, if available, is paid as a preloaded gift card sent to the email address provided. All parties acknowledge that no goods or services were received or provided in exchange for union membership, that they acted voluntarily, and that money received reflects a reimbursement of future membership dues to be collected by LIUNA Local 1776.
