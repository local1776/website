Who We Are

LIUNA Local 1776 represents U.S. government employees, including National Guard technicians and other civilian employees of the Department of Defense. We are a local of the Laborers’ International Union of North America.

Our Union

A Local of LIUNA

Since its founding in 1903, the Laborers’ International Union of North America has represented working women and men in the United States and Canada, in every sector of employment, public and private. With more than 500,000 members, LIUNA is one of the largest unions in North America, and it uses that collective strength to make our members’ voices heard by lawmakers at home and in Washington.

Local 1776 is part of LIUNA’s Pacific Southwest Region. Our mission is to represent U.S. government employees, including the employees of the Department of Defense and the Army and Air National Guard: in the workplace, at the bargaining table and on Capitol Hill.

Our History

From Many Locals to One

  1. 1998

    LIUNA Arrives

    LIUNA begins representing National Guard technicians. Most states form their own local; Louisiana has three.

  2. 2011

    A District Council

    LIUNA creates the National Guard District Council to oversee the Guard locals.

  3. 2015

    Local 1776

    LIUNA dissolves the district council and merges the locals into one: Local 1776.

  4. Today

    One Local

    Guam, Nevada and the Professional Education Center have since joined Local 1776 directly.
